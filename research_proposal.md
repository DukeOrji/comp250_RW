Research Proposal | Comp250 (Research Writing)

part1
My research will analyze the ethical concerns surrounding automated content moderation on social media platforms. Specifically, the obstacles these systems encounter when identifying harmful content while avoiding the removal of legitimate content. This limitation raises questions like: Can social media platforms use automated moderation systems to reduce harmful content without unnecessarily hindering users from expressing themselves? The main issue is whether these systems are reliable enough to relinquish sole autonomy for content moderation, especially because moderation systems find it difficult to accurately process sarcasm, complex context and cultural differences. It’s important to keep this limitation in consideration because moderation decisions affect users’ freedom of expression, their ability to contribute to online communities, etc.
