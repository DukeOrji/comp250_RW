Title: Limitations of Automated Moderation

Quotation:
“offensive or misleading content can still slip through automated systems” (Weldon 2026).


Source:
Weldon, David. 2026. “6 types of AI content moderation and how they work.” TechTarget, March 25, 2026. https://www.techtarget.com/enterprise-software/tip/6-types-of-AI-content-moderation-and-how-they-work


Commentary:
Even when automated moderation is used as the first layer of defense, it can never be assured that all posts with malicious content are filtered out. This gives a chance for the malicious content to slip through and evade moderation altogether, reinforcing the need for continuous evaluation and human intervention.


Keywords: Automated moderation, evasion, harmful content,
